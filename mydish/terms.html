2. Free scans

  • Each device gets 3 free scans.
  • When a pass ends, your free scans are refilled.
  • A scan that reads no dishes, or that stops because the menu has too much text, is not counted.

3. Passes

  • A pass lasts 1 week or 1 month from the moment you buy it. The price is shown in the app before you buy.
  • Passes do not renew automatically. Nothing is charged when a pass ends.
  • If you buy another pass before the current one ends, the new time is added to the end.
  • While a pass is active you can scan up to 15 times a day. One scan reads up to 40 dishes.
  • The app tells you when your pass is within 2 days of ending.

Refunds are handled by the App Store or Google Play under their policies, and requests are made to them directly. If a purchase is refunded, the pass ends.
