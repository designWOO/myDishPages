1. Who we are

myDish is a mobile app that reads a restaurant menu with your camera and describes each dish in your language. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. Contact: imdoubleuu@gmail.com

2. Information the app uses

WhatWhenWhy and whereStored?
Menu photoWhen you tap ScanSent securely through our server to our AI provider (Anthropic) to read the dish names and write descriptionsNot stored after processing
Dish name and languageWhen you tap a dish name to hear itSent to our server to create the pronunciation audioNot stored

A menu photo may include whatever is in front of the camera. Please avoid photographing people or personal documents.

Your IP address is processed to deliver responses and to limit abuse of free scans. The related count is deleted automatically within a few days, as are short-lived scan records.

3. Service providers

We use Amazon Web Services (AWS) in the United States to run the app’s server and store the records above. Pronunciation audio is created with Amazon Polly. Menu photos are sent to Anthropic in the United States to be read by its AI model, Claude. Anthropic processes the photo to return the result and does not use it to train its models; it may keep it for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring under its privacy policy. If that service is unavailable, the same kind of model may be used through Amazon Bedrock on AWS. Sign-in is provided by Apple or Google, and purchases are processed by the App Store or Google Play. These providers process data only to deliver the feature you requested.

4. Links that leave the app

“Images” opens a Google Images search for the dish name, and a saved restaurant opens in Google Maps. When you use these, Google’s own privacy policy applies. Your device’s system services may be used to turn your location into a place name.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your information for advertising.
  • We do not build a profile of you or track you across apps and websites.
  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, or payment card details.

6. Your choices

  • Camera and location: you can turn either permission off in your device Settings. Scanning needs the camera; location is optional.
  • Your list: remove dishes with the trash icon, or delete the app to erase everything stored on your device.
  • Your account: tap the account icon at the top of the camera screen (or “Pass & account” at the bottom of your list), then “Delete account”. After you confirm with Apple, this removes your account record and any pass linked to it from our server and also removes the Sign in with Apple link to myDish.
  • Your device record: to ask us to delete the usage record tied to your device, email us with the device ID shown at the bottom of the account screen.

7. Children

myDish is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email imdoubleuu@gmail.com.

3. 처리 위탁 업체

앱의 서버와 위 기록의 저장은 미국에 있는 Amazon Web Services(AWS)에서 이루어집니다. 발음 음성은 Amazon Polly로 만듭니다. 메뉴 사진은 미국에 있는 Anthropic으로 전송되어 AI 모델 Claude가 읽습니다. Anthropic은 결과를 돌려주기 위해서만 사진을 처리하며 AI 학습에 사용하지 않고, 안전과 남용 방지를 위해 자체 개인정보 처리방침에 따라 일정 기간 보관할 수 있습니다. 이 서비스를 쓸 수 없을 때는 AWS의 Amazon Bedrock을 통해 같은 종류의 모델을 사용할 수 있습니다. 로그인은 Apple 또는 Google이 제공하며, 구매는 App Store 또는 Google Play가 처리합니다. 이 업체들은 요청한 기능을 제공하는 목적으로만 정보를 처리합니다.

4. 앱 밖으로 연결되는 기능

“이미지”는 메뉴 이름으로 Google 이미지 검색을 열고, 저장한 식당은 Google 지도에서 열립니다. 이 기능을 사용할 때는 Google의 개인정보 처리방침이 적용됩니다. 위치를 장소 이름으로 바꾸는 데 기기의 시스템 서비스가 사용될 수 있습니다.

  • 리스트: 휴지통 아이콘으로 메뉴를 지우거나, 앱을 삭제하면 기기에 저장된 내용이 지워집니다.

  • 기기 기록: 기기에 연결된 사용 기록의 삭제를 원하시면, 계정 화면 맨 아래에 표시된 기기 번호를 적어 이메일로 요청해 주세요.
